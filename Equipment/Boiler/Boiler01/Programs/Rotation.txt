' Pump rotation control logic: Initializes with Pump 1 as lead, then automatically
' rotates to Pump 2 every Monday at 6:00 AM to balance runtime wear between pumps.
' Includes a 60-second delay after each rotation before resuming standby.

Numeric Public Lead_Boiler

Waiting_to_Rotate:
	If Weekday = 2 and TimeOfDay = 0600 Then Goto Rotation

Rotation:
	If Lead_Boiler = 1 Then 
		Lead_Boiler = 2 
	Else 
		Lead_Boiler = 1
	Endif
	Goto Rotation_Delay

Rotation_Delay:
	If TS > 60 Then Goto Waiting_to_Rotate
