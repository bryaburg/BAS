'ChWtrEna
'Built by JMartinez
'Version 2022-06-30 1104
'Opens 100% the Chilled Water valve

Numeric Input MechClgEna, AhuEna
Numeric Output ChWtrVlvPos

VlvClose:
	ChWtrVlvPos = 0
	If (MechClgEna = On And AhuEna = On) Then Goto VlvOpen

VlvOpen:
	ChWtrVlvPos = 100
	If (MechClgEna = Off or AhuEna = Off) Then Goto VlvClose
