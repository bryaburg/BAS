'Modulates the chilled water valve based upon circulation requirements

Numeric Input SaFlowSts, SaTmp, SaTmpSpt, MechClgEna
Numeric Output ChwVlvPos

ValveClosed:
ChwVlvPos = 0.0
If SaFlowSts is On and MechClgEna = On and Then goto ValveOpen

ValveOpen:
ChwVlvPos = 100
If SaFlowSts is Off or MechClgEna = Off then Goto DelayOff

DelayOff:
If SaFlowSts is On and MechClgEna = On and Then goto ValveOpen
If TS > 900 and (SaFlowSts is Off or MechClgEna = Off) then Goto ValveClosed
