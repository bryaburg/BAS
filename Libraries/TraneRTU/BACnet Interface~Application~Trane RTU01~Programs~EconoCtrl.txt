'EconoCtrl
'Built by Barbeau
'Version 2019-04-25 1059
'Controls Econimizer on outdoor air temp

Numeric Input OaTmp, EconoOaTmpEnaSpt
Numeric Output EconoEna

EconoOff:
EconoEna = Off
If TS > 120 and OaTmp < (EconoOaTmpEnaSpt - 1) Then Goto EconoOn

EconoOn:
EconoEna = On
If TS > 120 and OaTmp > (EconoOaTmpEnaSpt + 1) Then Goto EconoOff
