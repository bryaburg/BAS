These are the mulitstate values that are sent from the room unit device when each mode is selected.
Note that these do not match Hvac Modes.

SmartX stat HVAC mode output values
1 Auto (When device heating/cool mode has never been changed before)
2 Heat
3 Cool
4 Auto (When device has been changed from heat or cool)


HvacSts values
1 Occupied Cooling
2 Occupied Heating
3 Standby Cooling
4 Standby Heating
5 Setup
6 Setback
7 Off
8 Fire
