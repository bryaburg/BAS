'This program changes the Lead every week on Tuesday at 9:00am.

Numeric Input LgPmpFalm
Numeric Output ChgLd

CheckStatus:
  If Weekday = Tuesday and Hourofday > 9 and TD > 1 and LgPmpFalm = Off then Goto ChangeLeadOn

ChangeLeadOn:
  ChgLd = On
  If TS > 5 Then Goto ChangeLeadOff

ChangeLeadOff:
  ChgLd = Off
  Goto CheckStatus

E:
  If TS >= 10 then Goto CheckStatus
