'Fails chiller on high water temp
'Bind to BACnet points for water temp and water temp setpoint (if available)

Numeric Input ChwSTmp, ChwSTmpSpt, ChlrCmd
Numeric Output ChlrFalm

AlarmOff:
  ChlrFalm = On
  If ChwSTmp > (ChwSTmpSpt + 5) Then Goto Monitoring

Monitoring:
  If ChwStmp < (ChwSTmpSpt + 3) Then Goto AlarmOff
  If ChwSTmp > (ChwSTmpSpt + 5) and TS > 1200 Then Goto AlarmOn

AlarmOn:
  If ChwStmp < (ChwSTmpSpt + 3) Then Goto AlarmOff
