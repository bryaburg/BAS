'Built by Barbeau
'Version 2021-02-24 0819
'Controls DX based upon setpoint

Numeric Input SaFanSts, SaTmp, SaTmpSpt, DxStartDlyTm, DxStgDnTs, DxStgUpTs, DxMd
Numeric Output ClgStg1Cmd

Shutdown:
  ClgStg1Cmd = Off
  If TS > DxStartDlyTm and SaFanSts = On and DxMd = On and SaTmp > (SaTmpSpt + 2) Then Goto Stg1

Stg1:
  ClgStg1Cmd = On
  If (TS > 60 and DxMd = Off) or SaFanSts = Off Then Goto Shutdown
  If TS > DxStgDnTs and SaTmp < (SaTmpSpt - 2) Then Goto Shutdown
